Pick lockers & backtesting

Write the recipe. Test it. Let it find the picks.

Stack conditions on thousands of season-tagged stats, then run the recipe against real past seasons to see how often it actually won, before you ride it. Once you're happy, the locker scans every slate for games that match.

  • Build three ways: quick TLDR, the full wizard, or by chatting with the Trainer
  • Backtest up to 20 seasons deep, depending on your level
  • Automatic picks from your rules, with no limit on how many
AI sports buddy

Ask anything. Get the numbers, not guesses.

Your buddy runs on our MacRAG knowledge system on every level. Ask who's hot, who's hurt or who led the league last week, and it answers from real data.

  • Pick a personality: the Hype Man, the Analyst, the Skeptic and more
  • It remembers your teams and habits
  • A daily brief on your teams, your picks and today's slate
Scores, stats & trends

Every game and every stat, all in one place.

Live scores and box scores, matchup previews, injuries and 600+ seasons of player and team history, with advanced stats and trend charts that show who's actually heating up.

  • Live scores across every league
  • Top players, rankings and standings for every team
  • Advanced stats and last-5 / last-10 trends
Reports

The quick read before every game.

Plain-language reports built fresh every night: what to know before a matchup, which way a player is trending, and whether your own picks are good or just lucky.

  • Game, player, team and league reports
  • Your own numbers: My Picks and Team Line reports
  • An add-on pack for any paid level
