How it works

A pick system you can test before you trust it

Most apps hand you someone else's picks. Mac's Sports Playground gives you the tools to build your own, check them against real seasons, and let them run.

01 · Write

Write the recipe

Stack conditions on thousands of season-tagged stats. Build it three ways: a quick TLDR, the full wizard, or by chatting with the Trainer.

02 · Test

Backtest it against past seasons

Run the recipe against real past seasons, up to 20 deep depending on your level, and see how often it actually won before you ride it.

03 · Run

Let the locker find the picks

Once you're happy, the locker scans every slate and surfaces every game that matches.

Backtesting

See how your recipe would have done. Before you trust it.

Every locker can be run against real past seasons before it ever makes a pick. You get the record, the win rate, ROI and units, an edge score, and the full game-by-game log, so you know exactly what you are looking at.

Record & win rateWins and losses across every past game the recipe matched.
ROI & unitsWhat the recipe returned over the whole run, not just its best week.
Edge scoreOne number that weighs line value, win rate and volume, with the breakdown shown.
Game logEvery matched game with the date, the pick, the result and the units it returned.

A recipe that would have lost is worth knowing about before it makes a pick, not after.
