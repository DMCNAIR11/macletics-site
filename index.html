How it works

A pick system you can test before you trust it

1

Write the recipe

Stack conditions on thousands of season-tagged stats. Build it with a quick TLDR, the full wizard, or by chatting with the Trainer.

2

Backtest it

Run the recipe against real past seasons, up to 20 deep depending on your level, and see how often it actually won.

3

Let the locker run

The locker scans every slate and surfaces every game that matches your rules.

Backtesting

See how your recipe would have done. Before you trust it.

Every locker can run against real past seasons before it makes a single pick. A recipe that would have lost is worth knowing about before it picks, not after.
