The four levels

Start free. Level up when you want more.

Every level gets the full AI buddy, stats for every team and player, and pick lockers. Higher levels go deeper.

Role Player

Free · ad-supported
  • AI buddy at Know-It-All level, powered by MacRAG
  • 600+ seasons of player & team stats (ad-supported)
  • 2 lockers with 150+ recipe stat fields
  • 2-season team backtests
  • 1 slip scan a day (beta)

Baller

Ad-free
  • Wizard buddy with standard memory
  • Advanced stats & trends, every season
  • 10 lockers, 1,000+ recipe fields, player & team props
  • 5-season team & 3-season player backtests
  • Can add Reports, DFS Pass & Pick Champion

The Franchise

Ad-free
  • Deity buddy with advanced memory, about 2,000 chats a month
  • EV% on every price, de-vigged
  • Contracts & personalized news
  • 30 lockers with 2,000+ recipe fields
  • 10-season team backtests

Capper's Delight

Ad-free · everything
  • Everything in The Franchise, plus the +EV Finder board
  • About 6,000 chats a month and maximum buddy memory
  • 75 lockers with 2,500+ recipe fields
  • 20-season team backtests
  • Team payrolls & league salary ranks · Pick Champion included

Every feature

What's inside

The chip on each card shows the first level that gets the feature. Every level above it gets it too.

01

Sports data & live games

Live scores & box scores

Free

Real-time scores, full box scores and play-by-play. Free members watch a short ad to open a live game.

Matchup previews

Free

Head-to-head history, recent form, injuries and the numbers that decide a game.

Player & team profiles

Free

Careers, game logs, season stats, schedules and rankings across 600+ seasons of history on every level. On Free, watch a quick ad to open seasons beyond the latest three.

Injury tracker

Free

Who's in, out or questionable across every team, wired into matchups.

Advanced stats & trends

Baller

Efficiency and usage metrics, plus last-5, last-10 and season splits that show how a player is actually trending.

Salary & contracts

Franchise

Multi-year contracts on every player. Capper's Delight adds team payroll pages and league-wide salary ranks.

02

Odds & analysis

Odds boards

Free

Game and player lines from the major books side by side, so you can see the best number at a glance.

Line history & futures

Free

Open-to-close line movement, plus championship, MVP and win-total markets.

Prediction-market prices

Free

Today's games with each prediction market's price for the winner side by side, and the gap between the highest and the lowest. Sort by the biggest disagreement to see where the markets split.

Pick tracking

Free

Log singles and parlays, get them graded automatically, and keep a full win-loss record.

Photo slip scanning

Beta Free · 21+

Snap a photo of a slip and every leg is filled in for you to review. 1 scan a day on Free, rising to 10, 20 and 30 a day by level. In beta, so check each leg before you save.

EV% on every price

Franchise

De-vigged expected value on every price, measured against the sharp reference book you choose.

+EV Finder board

Capper's

The whole slate scanned for positive-edge picks, measured against a blend of the sharpest books rather than any single one. Sorted by edge, filterable by sport, market and book.

03

Lockers & backtesting

Pick lockers

Free

Your own pick systems. Write the recipe your picks must match, and the locker finds them every day, for every game that qualifies. Build one three ways: quick TLDR, the full wizard, or by chatting with the Trainer. Picks you add by hand are limited by level.

Recipe stat fields

Free

Build on 150+ season-tagged stat fields on Free, then 1,000+, 2,000+ and 2,500+ as you level up.

Props lockers

Baller

Build systems around player and team props, not just sides and totals.

Backtest Lab

Free

Run any recipe against real past seasons before you trust it: team backtests go 2 seasons on Free, then 5, 10 and 20. Player backtests start at Baller and run 3 to 5 seasons.

Locker marketplace

Free

Publish your lockers, see how others rank, and follow the ones you like with subscription coins.

Locker appearance

Free

Colors, themes, icons and skins to make every locker look like yours.

04

Your AI buddy

Ask anything

Free

An AI Buddy powered by our MacRAG knowledge system on every level. Answers come from real data, not guesses. Chat allowances are approximate and depend on the question. On Free, a quick ad every 10 messages keeps the chat going.

Brain levels

Free

Know-It-All on Free, Wizard at Baller, Deity at The Franchise and up. Smarter answers and more tools as you climb.

Memory

Baller

Your buddy remembers your teams, players and habits. Standard memory at Baller, advanced at The Franchise, maximum at Capper's Delight.

Buddy characters

Free

Start with a free buddy and unlock more characters in the Store, each with its own art and personality.

Daily brief

Free

A personal rundown of your teams, your picks and today's slate.

Daily Deck

Free

A face-down deck of cards waits when you first open the app each day. Tap to flip, swipe for the next card, and pick up where you left off if you close it. One card is a quick survey.

Personalized news

Franchise

Everyone gets the daily news feed. At The Franchise you tune it to your teams, players and picks.

05

Coins, social & your home screen

Coin wallet

Free

Earn MAC coins from missions and activity. Backtest coins refill every day, and subscription coins let you follow marketplace lockers.

Friends & chat

Free

Friend requests, direct messages and group chat rooms.

Your dashboard

Free

Pin shortcuts to the tools you use most. Capper's Delight gets a bigger grid.

Add-on packs

Go deeper on one thing

Packs add a focused toolset on top of any paid level. You add them from the Store in the app.

Reports

Pack

Plain-language reports on games, players, teams, the league and your own picks. Higher levels open more reports: line movement at The Franchise; hot over/unders, situational and prop hit-rate reports at Capper's Delight.

Baller and up

Pick Champion

Pack

Your buddy's analyst mode. Every pick gets a verdict, every player a hot-streak read, and every parlay a score, right in the chat.

Baller and up · included with Capper's Delight

DFS Pass

Beta

A DraftKings and FanDuel value board that ranks every player by projected points per salary dollar, plus a lineup optimizer with locks, fades and stacks.
